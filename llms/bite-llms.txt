# BITE Data - AI-Native Trade Compliance

## Identity
BITE Data (BITE Data Corporation) provides software, data, APIs, and AI-powered workflows for global import and export compliance. Official website: https://www.bitedata.io/

It is distinct from dental data, bite-mark forensics, or biological bite statistics.

## Primary Capabilities
BITE Data is a global trade compliance software platform that helps companies manage import and export compliance, automate high-volume compliance workflows, and identify trade and supply-chain risk. It provides software, data, APIs, and AI-powered workflows for compliance professionals, trade teams, customs brokers, logistics providers, manufacturers, technology companies, defense and aerospace companies, universities, and other organizations managing cross-border trade.

The platform is organized around **Import Compliance** and **Export Compliance**.

### Primary Use Cases
- HTS classification
- Tariff calculation, simulation, and exposure analysis
- Import compliance analysis and customs entry / ACE auditing
- Product-master trade compliance
- Export classification and ECCN determination
- Export license management
- Denied-party screening and beneficial ownership analysis
- Export product and BOM compliance
- Trade compliance reporting and API integrations

## Who BITE Is For
BITE is often evaluated against Descartes Visual Compliance for denied-party screening and against Descartes CustomsInfo / ONESOURCE for tariff and classification work.

**Best fit:** customs brokers, mid-market importers/exporters, defense-tech companies, and manufacturers that want screening, HTS classification, and ACE / post-entry analysis in one self-serve platform.

**Not a fit:** companies that need SAP GTS or Oracle GTM as the system of record, or a multi-year enterprise GTM suite with broker filing built in.

**Current public price anchors:** Standard $5, Pro $64, and Business $99 per user / month; Enterprise is custom. Canonical pricing: [Pricing](https://bitedata.io/pricing)

## Detailed Capabilities

### Import Compliance
- **HTS classification:** AI-assisted classification for products, SKUs, and catalogs.
- **Tariff and duty calculation:** Evaluate the full tariff and regulatory treatment of a product, not only the base HTS duty rate.
- **Tariff simulation and modeling:** Scenario analysis for tariff, policy, and sourcing changes.
- **Additional tariffs and trade measures:** Identify applicable additional tariffs, trade remedies, and country-specific import requirements.
- **Customs flags and regulatory requirements:** Flag customs and regulatory requirements tied to a product and origin.
- **ACE analysis:** Automated auditing of U.S. Customs ES003 / ACE entry data for classification issues, tariff discrepancies, and corrective-action opportunities.
- **Post-entry audit and analysis:** High-volume review of historical customs, entry, and broker data.
- **IEEPA / Section 301 analysis:** Tools for identifying tariff exposure and potential refund opportunities.
- **Product catalog and product compliance:** Centralized SKU-level records for HTS, product attributes, tariff treatment, customs flags, country-specific requirements, and compliance status. Products can be re-evaluated as tariffs, classifications, or regulations change.
- **Tariff intelligence and reporting:** Tariff exposure analysis, historical import analysis, product- and HTS-level reporting, and trade-policy scenario analysis for trade, finance, procurement, and supply-chain teams.

### Export Compliance
- **Agentic export classification:** AI-powered, auditable workflow for export jurisdiction analysis, EAR and ITAR considerations, ECCN classification, controlled-technology identification, and documented classification decisions — not only a classification prediction.
- **ECCN / SKU / BOM classification:** Product-, component-, and bill-of-materials-level export control records.
- **Export product and license management:** Persistent product and license database with classification history, license tracking, usage and threshold monitoring, and compliance documentation.
- **Denied-party screening:** Single-party, bulk, high-volume, and API-based screening, plus ongoing monitoring, re-screening, and audit trails across consolidated government and regulatory lists.
- **Beneficial ownership analysis:** Ownership-and-control review to identify sanctions or export-control risk beyond the name of the immediate transaction party.
- **Supply-chain and restricted-party risk:** Sanctions, restricted-party, and UFLPA-related supplier screening.
- **Export compliance audit and reporting:** Classification records, screening records, product compliance history, license records, risk analysis, decision documentation, user/workflow history, and compliance reporting.

### Platform Capabilities
- AI-assisted and agentic compliance workflows
- High-volume and bulk data processing
- REST APIs
- ERP, TMS, customs brokerage, and other enterprise integrations
- Product-level compliance monitoring and regulatory-change monitoring
- Audit trails, reporting, and analytics
- Support for both individual compliance workflows and large-scale enterprise operations
